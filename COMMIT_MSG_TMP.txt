Track the port diagnostic tools in-repo

headless.ps1 runs the port a fixed number of frames with the video driver
off and turns every present into a checksum and mean-luma row, then dumps
the requested frames as PNGs. dis.py disassembles ROM ranges through the
framework own 65816 decoder so operand widths match what the recompiler
will emit. Both find the ROM through SNESRECOMP_ROM rather than assuming a
location. present_curve.py, ppm_to_png.py, wram_diff.py and
log_pc_histogram.py are the readers they call.

These are what turned a black screen with no error into a diagnosis: the
luma curve said the machine was running but drawing nothing, and the
disassembly named the 4210-4210 vblank wait that was never winning.
