## Useful coverage connections

- Atland connects to Gold Cave; the Atland page also marks the church, shops, mayor and Place of Faith.
- Gold Cave marks Giles, Samson's locked approach, the Red Crystal and the exit to Elf Field.
- Ice Cave marks the Angel's Bell event that leads toward Phantom Hill.
- The Cloud page marks the northern jump toward Lagoon Castle.
- Lagoon Castle marks the statue/Felicia passage, Battler and the jump toward Secret Place.

Verify each connection through original loading, flags and gameplay before recording a new campaign result. Map-image crops can differ from the full engine room extent; visual dimensions alone do not identify a ROM room. The static catalogue still has two records without ordinary incoming doors (2A and 3F); their entry classifications require source/runtime proof.
