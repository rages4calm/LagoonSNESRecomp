# Lagoon — X68000 soundtrack

Open **Lagoon (X68000).m3u8** in your music player, or play any of the 40 MP3 files directly. Track names and order follow the original VGMRips package, including its nine unused tracks.

These listening copies use LAME quality 2 variable bitrate, joint stereo, 44.1 kHz. Each contains the original VGM's single pass: the introductory section followed by one loop where present, with no extra repetitions or fade. Tracks 29 (*Item Get*) and 31 (*Say ZOOM!*) are one-shots. Some players may include a tiny amount of MP3 encoder padding at the end.

The game uses a separate lossless PCM pack with the original sample-accurate loop points. These MP3 files are for listening and are not required to play. `tracks.json` records track names, durations, source hashes, loop information and export hashes.

Music: **Hideki Suzuki**, original game by **ZOOM** (1990). VGMRips package: **GTheGuardian**; optimization: **Valley Bell** (2017). The original music remains the work of its creators and is separate from the port's source-code license.
